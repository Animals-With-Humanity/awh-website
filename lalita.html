Lalita's story

Lalita is a young street dog who was found after falling from the 4th floor of an under-construction building. The impact shattered the life she had - both front legs were badly injured, and she needed immediate veterinary care just to survive the night.

Since then, Animals With Humanity has been with her through dressings, scans, pain management, and the long wait for surgery. She is still in recovery: cone on, legs supported, and completely dependent on people who chose not to look away.

We are urgently raising ₹45,000 so Lalita can get the surgery and aftercare she needs. This is not just an operation - it is weeks of medicines, regular dressing, food, and a safe foster home while she learns to walk again.

Bills will be provided. Every donation is for Lalita's treatment and recovery.

Where ₹45,000 goes

  • Surgery and hospital charges
  • Post-operative care and pain relief
  • Medicines and regular dressing
  • Food while she cannot forage
  • Safe fostering until she can walk

Treatment bills will be shared with donors on request.
