Be kind, to all kinds.

Animals With Humanity is a youth-led NGO in Bhopal. We want a city where animals and people live together in peace. We help street animals through adoption and vaccination, and we teach people how to care for them.

Pawse is our festival. For three days, paw parents, students, artists and small brands meet in one place to adopt, play, learn and shop sustainably. Everything is vegan, and every paw is welcome.
