Be kind, to all kinds.

Animals With Humanity is a grassroots, youth-led movement that works to ensure animals live with dignity, freedom and safety. We rescue and rehabilitate injured and abandoned animals, run sterilisation and vaccination drives, intervene in cruelty cases, promote plant-based living, and build compassionate communities through awareness and education.

We believe in true coexistence — a world where animals and humans can live peacefully, and where animals are not seen as property, but as lives that matter.

PAWSE is a pet-friendly community festival that brings people, animals and the planet together. From animal adoption and paw-friendly activities to vegan food, sustainable brands, music, performances, games and shopping, PAWSE brings together people from different communities to celebrate, connect and have a good time.

Whether you're a paw parent, an animal lover, vegan, sustainability enthusiast or simply someone looking for a fun day out, PAWSE has a place for you.
